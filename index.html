When they push back

Answer with facts from this page only. If you don’t know, say you’ll find out — don’t guess.

“That’s too expensive.”

The usual downsell is the 1-Week Sprint: $299 one time, 7 days to test the workflow. They pick 5 simple short-form videos, 2–3 complex short-form videos, or 1 long-form video. No auto-renewal. If they only need one video, Single Edit is $149 once, with no subscription and no contract.

“Can I just try it first?”

Yes — that is what the 1-Week Sprint is for. $299, 7 days, no auto-renewal. If they like it, they can book another week any time or move to Unlimited.

“How fast do I get it?”

Short-form, simple or complicated, is 1–4 days. Long-form is 5–7 days for a YouTube video up to 25 minutes, and 2 weeks for a full production. Short-form means 90 seconds or under.

How you get paid

On a subscription client, you earn 25% of what’s left after Stripe fees in the first month. After that it drops to 20% a month, to maintain the client, and runs for as long as you retain them as project manager. On one-time packages you earn 20% of the sale.

What each package pays you

The amount after Stripe fees is the price × 0.91. That factor is fixed for every package: it’s what’s left after the 9% Stripe service fee and conversion-rate fee.

What keeps it rolling

  • The month finishes and the client rolls over into the next month.
  • You stay on as that client’s project manager and step in when something is out of the ordinary.
  • If a client is reassigned to you, you get that month’s commission prorated from when they were assigned to you.

When and how you’re paid

  • Commission is paid every two weeks, after the client’s deposit hits our account.
  • We don’t charge tax on our service, and we don’t offer refunds. A client who wants to test the service uses the 1-week trial (Sprint).
  • Your sale is credited when you post the hand-off in the shared closes channel.

Who gets the commission when two reps touch one lead

The second rep gets it, if…

A rep reached out to a lead but:

  • hadn’t officially made contact, or
  • the lead was dropped, or
  • the rep wasn’t able to sell to them,

…and another rep picks up the account and closes it. The commission goes to the second rep.

Managing clients in Slack

Project managers can’t create channels — only the owner can. Everything about closes lives in one shared channel where all project managers post updates. Keep it short: a simple confirmation is enough.

  1. 1

    One shared channel

    All project managers post updates on closes here. This is where everything is.

  2. 2

    Post the hand-off when a client closes

    Use the hand-off template: client and business name, correct email and phone, what was purchased, the start date they want, and your name.

  3. 3

    Keep threads short

    No detail needed. A one-line confirmation is enough, like “Confirmed” or “Got it”. No checkmarks or emoji.

  4. 4

Precautions: spotting a client who may leave

A client who leaves ends the commission, so catch the signs early. Everyone in the channel has a part. On Unlimited a client can cancel up to 15 days before the next billing cycle, so a hint of leaving is time-sensitive.

Warning signs — tag the project manager if you see any

  • The client mentions cancelling, pausing or leaving.
  • The same video keeps going back for revisions.
  • The client’s tone turns frustrated, or they say it isn’t what they asked for.
  • Replies slow down, or the channel goes quiet.
  • Requests stop coming in (an empty queue on Unlimited).
  • A delivery is running past its turnaround window.

Clients

Tell them at onboarding.

  • If anything feels off, @ the project manager in the channel. No need to wait.
  • If you’re thinking of leaving, say so to the project manager first so they can help.

Editors

You talk to the client most, so you see problems first.

  • Tag the project manager as soon as you notice a warning sign. Early is better than late.
  • Don’t debate scope, pricing or cancelling with the client. Hand it over.
  • If you can’t hit a delivery window, say so and tag the project manager.

Project managers

You’re the first stop.

  • Acknowledge in the thread right away so the client knows someone has it.
  • Read the whole thread and check the package terms (revision rounds, delivery windows).
  • Fix it if it’s within scope. If it’s out of scope or the client plans to leave, @ Mohamed Nour.
  • Never promise refunds, discounts or exceptions yourself.
Client can see the channel? Keep the thread calm and short. Move sensitive details (price, cancelling, who’s at fault) to a DM or internal channel with the project manager and Mohamed Nour.

Rules of the road

Simple rules that keep clients, the owner and your commission safe.

Do

  • Call your assigned leads through the Quo dialer.
  • Quote the packages exactly as written in the packages section.
  • Send the payment link that matches the package they chose.
  • Post the hand-off in the shared closes channel as soon as a client closes, with correct contact details. Keep threads short.
  • Let the editor and client talk directly. Step in only when something is out of the ordinary.

  • @ Mohamed Nour whenever a client wants to incorporate AI.

Don’t

  • Promise results, guarantees or delivery times that aren’t listed.
  • Offer discounts or refunds, or quote a price that isn’t on this page.
  • Promise “same editor” on Single Edit or Sprint. It’s only listed on Unlimited.
  • Promise anything about AI work yourself. Hand it to Mohamed Nour.
  • Call Unlimited output a guarantee. 12–30 short-form or 4–8 long-form is an average.
  • Contact a lead that isn’t yours unless it’s been 3 days since the last activity and no follow-up note or meeting is in place.
  • Share this page outside the team. It contains commission terms.

FAQ

Who do I ask if I don’t know the answer?
Tag the project manager. If it’s out of scope, the project manager tags Mohamed Nour. You can also ask Mohamed to jump onto a call in Quo. For anything else, book time on the owner’s calendar. Don’t guess with a prospect.
How long do edits take?
Short-form (90 seconds or under), simple or complicated, is 1–4 days. Long-form (over 90 seconds) is 5–7 days for a YouTube video up to 25 minutes, and 2 weeks for a full production. See Turnaround at a glance.
Which payment link do I send?
The one for the package they chose: Single Edit ($149), 1-Week Sprint ($299) or Unlimited ($999/month). All three links are in the packages section with copy buttons.
The lead isn’t ready to decide on the call. What now?
Don’t push. Send your personal sales calendar link to book a follow-up, and leave a note on the contact in Quo with the next step.
The price is a problem. What do I offer?
Usually the 1-Week Sprint as a downsell: $299 one time, 7 days to test the workflow. If they only need one video, Single Edit is $149. Don’t invent discounts.
Another rep already reached out to a lead. Can I contact them?
Only if it’s been 3 days since the last activity and no follow-up note or meeting is in place. If the first rep never officially made contact, the lead was dropped, or they couldn’t sell to them, and you pick it up and close it, the commission goes to you. See the lead rules.
When do I earn commission, and when do I get paid?
On Unlimited you earn 25% of what’s left after Stripe fees in the first month ($999 × 0.91 × 25% = $227.27), then 20% a month ($181.82) for as long as you retain the client. On one-time packages you earn 20% of the sale. It’s paid every two weeks, after the deposit hits our account. If the client drops, it stops. See How you get paid.
A client wants to incorporate AI. What do I do?
Always @ Mohamed Nour, every time. Don’t promise anything yourself.
Can Mohamed join one of my calls?
Yes. Quo lets people jump onto a call, so if you need to ask Mohamed something or want him on the call, it’s possible.
A client says they’re thinking of leaving. What do I do?
Acknowledge it right away, read the thread, and don’t promise refunds, discounts or exceptions. If you can’t resolve it within scope, tag Mohamed Nour using the escalation template, with what’s happening and what you’ve tried. On Unlimited, a client can cancel up to 15 days before the next billing cycle, so don’t wait.
